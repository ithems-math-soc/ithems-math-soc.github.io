Yohsuke Murase, Yuma Fujimoto, and Hisashi Ohtsuki have published a review article titled “The Mathematics of Reputation-Based Cooperation” (評判にもとづく協力行動の数理) in the October 2026 issue of Butsuri, the journal of the Physical Society of Japan.

The article introduces the mathematical foundations of indirect reciprocity and explains how reputation-based mechanisms can sustain cooperation in social dilemmas.
